# Codex brief: shunt transformer improvement

**Scope:** Tests-only research on the experimental shunt transformer in
[`TransformerModule.h`](../source/modules/TransformerModule.h). This follows the
module spike reported in [Architecture.md](Architecture.md) and the Step 1
diagnosis in [ShuntImprovement.md](ShuntImprovement.md). The shipped DSP,
editor, host state, protected tolerances and `JUCE/` / `modules/` are not edited.

## Owner decisions already made

- **DECISION:** the series topology is dropped. Do not fix, measure or render it.
- **DECISION:** work continues on shunt only, variants A and B.
- **DECISION (revision 3):** the shunt module's reference transformer is the
  **Carnhill VTB1148**. Calibration targets come from published VTB1148 data.
  This module is the **output transformer** stage. The input transformer is a
  later, separate phase and is out of scope here.
- **DECISION (revision 3):** level reference is **0 dBFS = +18 dBu**. So
  +4 dBu = −14 dBFS and 0 dBu = −18 dBFS. Use this mapping everywhere a
  published dBu figure meets a digital level.
- **DECISION (revision 3):** the shipped Preisach stage is **no longer a
  research reference**. Do not render it, calibrate to it or compare CPU
  against it. Its existing regression tests (bit-exact baseline, latency
  schedule) stay and must keep passing, because the plugin still ships it.
  Borrowing implementation techniques from its code (Step 4) is fine.
- **DECISION:** knee shape and HF roll-off are **ON HOLD** (Step 3).
- **OPEN:** CPU budget (percent of one core, 48 kHz stereo, transformer stage)
  and latency budget (samples at 48 kHz). Steps 4 and 5 report against these
  once supplied; until then, report raw numbers only.

## Step 1 — DONE: aliasing versus harmonics

Reported in [ShuntImprovement.md](ShuntImprovement.md). Findings that shape
this brief:

- The "harsh high boost" in the spike's shunt renders was not generated by the
  model: the synthetic bass input already has broadband energy from
  note-boundary steps, and RMS-matched A/B renders are within ±0.2 dB of the
  unprocessed input in every octave band. Synthetic stimuli are therefore not
  used for listening from here on.
- Aliasing is negligible: A at the numerical floor; B's small non-harmonic
  component falls with factor and sits ~45 dB under its harmonics at 4x.
- Off-grid screen failures were short-conditioning leakage; periodic exports
  now condition for one second.
- At current defaults the prototype is very clean (55 Hz, peak 0.8, 20–250 Hz
  harmonics: A −61 dBFS, B −51 dBFS at 0 dB drive). Its drive scale has no
  defined relation to any real level yet.

## Step 2 — MEASUREMENT: calibrate to VTB1148 published data

### 2a. Collect and cite the reference data

Build a reference table in `docs/measurements/shunt_improvement/vtb1148_reference.csv`
with one row per published data point: quantity, value, unit, frequency, level,
test conditions (winding configuration, source, load, driver), source document,
page/figure, and whether it is a read-off-a-plot estimate.

Sources:

- Louis Woods, *The Measurement of Audio Transformer Colouration* (2019),
  University of Huddersfield thesis. Starting points are already summarized in
  [TransformerVoicing.md](TransformerVoicing.md#published-vtb1148-starting-references),
  with page numbers. Use Appendix D (pp. 135–140) for additional spectra and
  distortion-versus-level/frequency plots.
- The Carnhill VTB1148 datasheet: page 2 of
  [Carnhill Design Guide, Issue 1e](references/Carnhill-Design-Guide-Issue1e.pdf)
  (Audio Maintenance Ltd). It gives only:
  - type: gapped, high-level balanced line **output** transformer
    (the Neve 1073/1272 line output position, Neve part LO1166);
  - turns ratio N1:N2 = 1+1 : 1.7+1.7;
  - DC coil resistances P1:P2:S1:S2 = 6 : 6 : 20 : 20 ohms;
  - optimum source/load impedances and voltage gain:

    | Primaries | Secondaries | Source | Load | Gain |
    |---|---|---:|---:|---:|
    | series | series | 200 Ω | 600 Ω | +4 dB |
    | series | parallel | 200 Ω | 150 Ω | −2 dB |
    | parallel | series | 50 Ω | 600 Ω | +10 dB |
    | parallel | parallel | 50 Ω | 150 Ω | +4 dB |

  It gives **no** distortion, frequency response, maximum level or
  inductance figures; those come from the Woods thesis only. Use the
  series/series (200 Ω : 600 Ω, 1:1.7) configuration, which matches the
  thesis test setup. **Never fill a value from memory or an unnamed source.**

Respect the caveats already recorded: the thesis used series windings (1:1.7),
a specific active driver and a 100 kOhm analyzer load; high-gain LF points
affected by driver failure are excluded; plot readings are estimates.

### 2b. Map circuit values

Using datasheet impedances, winding resistance and the thesis test conditions,
set the shunt circuit's `sourceOhms`, `loadOhms` and `loss`, referred to the
model's ideal unity-ratio secondary. Document every conversion (ratio
referral, normalization by the 1000 ohm impedance base). Where the data does
not determine a value, say so and keep the current default.

### 2c. Fit using existing parameters only

Allowed to change: `driveCalibrationDb`, and values of the existing descriptors
`saturation`, `knee`, `memory` (B only), `loss`, plus source/load from 2b.
Ranges may be widened if a fit needs it; report any widening.
Not allowed: new constitutive curves, circuit changes, solver or stopping-rule
changes, the `fluxScale` / linear-slope design constants, any added filter.

Fit A and B separately against:

1. **THD versus level** at the published frequencies (at minimum 20 Hz, plus
   any others the data gives), across the published level range, converted
   with 0 dBFS = +18 dBu.
2. **THD versus frequency** at the published level(s): real iron distorts
   more as frequency falls, and the fit must be checked across frequency,
   not tuned at one point.
3. **Small-signal LF response:** magnitude and phase at 20 Hz against the
   published values.

Report fit quality per data point (model value, published value, difference).
Do not hide misfits by weighting. Report explicitly, as findings for the
owner, anything the existing parameters cannot reach, including at least:

- **even harmonics:** the published part shows odd and even components; the
  current symmetric curve cannot produce even harmonics;
- **LF corner/phase,** if `fluxScale` prevents matching both LF response and
  saturation onset at once;
- any level where the fitted model and published THD diverge by more than
  a factor of 2 (6 dB).

**Define "drive" after calibration:** 0 dB drive means the calibrated VTB1148
operating point at the +18 dBu reference. Drive above 0 dB is extra gain into
the iron (compensated at the output, as now). Report the fitted
`driveCalibrationDb` and all fitted parameter values in a table.

### 2d. Renders on the existing example material

**DECISION:** use the existing synthetic material from Step 1 (bass, drums,
vocal-like, and the log sweep). Real recordings will be run by the owner once
the model is in the live build; no recordings directory is used here.
The synthetic bass has broadband note-boundary steps (Step 1), so every set
must include the unprocessed input for comparison.

For each material, scale it so its RMS is −18 dBFS (0 dBu, nominal), and
render at 8x:

- input;
- A and B calibrated, at 0 dB drive (nominal level);
- A and B at +12 dB drive (program around +12 dBu into the iron);
- A and B at +18 dB drive (hot, around +18 dBu).

RMS-match each material's set after rendering. Report per file: octave-band
difference from input, distortion-residual-to-output RMS ratio (residual =
output minus a gain- and delay-matched linear fit of the input), solver
iteration maxima and failures, input-limit hits.

Write to `docs/measurements/shunt_improvement/step2_audio/`, e.g.
`vocal_A_shunt_8x_vtb1148_drive+12dB.wav`. Rendered excerpts may be committed.
No sound verdicts in the report.

**Stop gate:** report the reference table, circuit mapping, fitted parameters,
per-point fit quality, the list of what cannot be matched, and the renders.
Do not proceed.

## Step 3 — ON HOLD: knee shape and HF roll-off

Not authorized. After the owner listens at the calibrated drive, they may
authorize either or both, using the VTB1148 data as targets:

- **Knee / curve:** a constitutive-curve option alongside `tanh` (rounder
  curve, and an asymmetric option if Step 2 shows even harmonics are needed),
  fitted to the published harmonic structure.
- **Roll-off:** a separate, visible second-order low-pass after the core
  standing in for leakage inductance and winding capacitance, with corner
  and Q taken from the published HF response, at the oversampled rate.

## Step 4 — PROPOSAL: solver cost

After the sound settles, port the shipped model's cost-reduction techniques
into the prototype, without changing its equations or stopping rule:

- Replace `std::tanh` in the hot path with a table like the shipped
  [`ConstitutiveLookup`](../source/experimental/ConstitutiveLookup.h).
- Cache committed operator values and skip re-evaluation for operators whose
  play position does not move, as in
  [`TransformerAudioCircuit.cpp`](../source/experimental/TransformerAudioCircuit.cpp).
- Return cached results when the first trial repeats the committed current.
- Remove virtual dispatch through `MagneticElement` from the per-iteration path.

Report CPU at 48 kHz stereo at the calibrated drive and at +18 dB, with
median, P99.9 and maximum, against the owner's CPU budget when supplied.
Confirm outputs are unchanged within the existing regression tolerance.

## Step 5 — MEASUREMENT: oversampling latency options

Only if the owner sets a latency budget lower than current. Measure a
minimum-phase FIR and an IIR half-band option against the current FIR at the
chosen factor: latency, passband ripple, image rejection, CPU.
